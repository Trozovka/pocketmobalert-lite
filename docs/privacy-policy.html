Privacy Policy for PocketMOBAlert

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This policy covers all versions of PocketMOBAlert: the free tier (Lite, open source), the paid tier (Pro, sold via Gumroad), and the Google Play Store build.

What this app does

PocketMOBAlert turns two or more phones into a man-overboard alert system over Bluetooth Low Energy. One phone (Crew mode) advertises a Bluetooth beacon; another (Watch mode) scans for it and sounds an alarm if it goes out of range unexpectedly.

What data this app collects

  • Bluetooth Low Energy identifiers: your phone's own beacon ID (Crew mode) and nearby paired crew devices' beacon IDs (Watch mode), used only to detect separation. Never transmitted anywhere — Bluetooth is entirely local, phone-to-phone.
  • Location: Android requires location permission to use Bluetooth LE scanning at all (an OS-level requirement, not something this app chooses). Location is also used to tag each alarm event in your local alert log with where it happened. Location data never leaves your device — there is no server, no analytics, no internet transmission of location data.
  • Purchase information (Pro / Play Store builds only): payment is handled entirely by Gumroad or Google Play Billing, respectively — this app never sees or stores your payment information, only whether the purchase was confirmed.

What this app does NOT do

  • No account creation, no sign-in, no user profiles.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • The free tier requests zero internet permission at all — the alarm works with no network dependency, full stop.

Data storage and deletion

All data (paired crew list, alert log, GPS positions) is stored locally on your device only, in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is nothing to request deletion of from us, because nothing is ever sent to us.
